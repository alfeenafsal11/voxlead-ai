# VoxLead CRM: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How quickly can our sales team onboard and deploy VoxLead CRM?
A: Standard setup for the Starter and Professional tiers takes less than 1 business day. For Enterprise deployments with custom HubSpot or Salesforce field mapping and historical data migration, typical onboarding is completed within 7 to 10 business days by your assigned Solutions Architect.

### Q: Does VoxLead work alongside our existing HubSpot or Salesforce CRM?
A: Yes. VoxLead operates either as a standalone sales CRM or as an intelligent autonomous voice and lead qualification layer on top of HubSpot and Salesforce. Changes, calls, contacts, and booked meetings sync bidirectionally in real time.

### Q: Can the voice agent speak languages other than English?
A: Yes, VoxLead natively supports real-time multilingual sales conversations in English and Hindi, maintaining the customer's preferred dialect and vocabulary throughout the call.

### Q: What happens if a prospective buyer asks a technical question not in the knowledge base?
A: The AI voice agent will never invent or guess product capabilities or technical specifications. It will politely inform the prospect that verified details are not available in its knowledge base and offer to log the requirement for discussion with a Solutions Architect during a scheduled demonstration.

### Q: Is customer conversation audio stored securely?
A: All audio streams and transcripts are encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We enforce strict role-based access control (RBAC), tenant data isolation, and customer-configurable data retention policies.
