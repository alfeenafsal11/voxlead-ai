# VoxLead CRM: Pricing & Subscription Plans

## Plan Tiers & Seat Pricing

### 1. Starter Tier
- **Price**: $29 per user per month billed annually ($35 billed monthly).
- **Minimum Seats**: 3 users.
- **Includes**:
  - Contact and lead management up to 5,000 active records.
  - Basic sales pipeline tracking with up to 3 custom deal stages.
  - Standard email synchronization with Gmail and Microsoft Outlook.
  - Standard voice call recording with 500 AI voice minutes per month.
  - Standard business hours email support.

### 2. Professional Tier (Most Popular)
- **Price**: $79 per user per month billed annually ($95 billed monthly).
- **Minimum Seats**: 5 users.
- **Includes**:
  - Up to 50,000 active contact and company records.
  - Unlimited custom sales pipelines, automated deal scoring, and stage gating.
  - Autonomous AI Voice Agent with 2,500 monthly conversational voice minutes.
  - Real-time RAG knowledge base integration and automated meeting booking.
  - Native two-way integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Google Calendar.
  - Advanced sentiment analysis, objection extraction, and automatic CRM note synchronization.
  - Priority support with guaranteed 4-hour response time SLA.

### 3. Enterprise Tier
- **Price**: $149 per user per month billed annually ($175 billed monthly).
- **Minimum Seats**: 20 users.
- **Annual Enterprise Contract**: For organizations with 40-50+ users, typical annual investment is around $50,000 to $70,000, including dedicated onboarding, customized CRM migration, and volume voice minute packages.
- **Includes**:
  - Unlimited contacts, accounts, and deal pipelines.
  - Unlimited or custom AI voice minutes with dedicated SIP trunking and low-latency telephony endpoints.
  - Multilingual voice agents supporting English, Spanish, Hindi, German, and French.
  - Custom data residency options, dedicated PostgreSQL clusters, and pgvector private indexing.
  - Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML 2.0 / Okta / Azure AD.
  - Dedicated Solutions Architect and 24/7/365 phone support with 99.99% uptime SLA.

## Billing Terms
- All contracts can be billed monthly or annually with a 20% annual discount.
- Payments accepted via corporate credit card, ACH, and wire transfer for invoices over $10,000.
