# VoxLead CRM: Sales Policies & Engagement Guidelines

## 1. Consultation & Proof of Concept Policy
- All prospective customers evaluating the Professional or Enterprise tier are eligible for a 14-day fully featured Proof of Concept (PoC) following a completed product demonstration with a sales engineer.
- We require confirmation of decision-maker presence on demonstration calls before activating custom sandbox environments.

## 2. Discounting & Payment Terms
- Annual upfront contracts receive an automatic 20% discount across all tiers.
- Multi-year enterprise agreements (2 or 3 years) qualify for additional volume discounts of up to 10% with executive finance approval.
- We do not offer one-off unapproved discounts during introductory calls; pricing is transparent and aligned with our published rate cards.

## 3. Data Privacy & Consent
- The voice agent must clearly notify callers or obtain explicit consent regarding call recording at the beginning of outbound or inbound interactions.
- Customer prospect data is never shared across tenant boundaries or used to train third-party foundation models.

## 4. Refund & Cancellation Terms
- Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time prior to the next billing cycle.
- Annual commitments include a 30-day money-back satisfaction guarantee during the initial rollout.
